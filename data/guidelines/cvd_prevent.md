# Cardiovascular Disease Risk — PREVENT (summary)

*Simplified educational summary prepared for this exercise. Not clinical guidance.*
Source: American Heart Association PREVENT equations (2023).

## What it estimates
The 10-year (and 30-year) risk of total cardiovascular disease — coronary heart
disease, stroke, and heart failure — in adults aged 30–79 without existing CVD.

## Risk factors used
- **Age** and **sex**.
- **Total cholesterol** and **HDL cholesterol**.
- **Systolic blood pressure**, and whether the patient is **on blood-pressure
  treatment**.
- **Statin use**.
- **Diabetes**.
- **Current smoking**.
- **Body mass index (BMI)**.
- **Kidney function (eGFR)** — a lower eGFR raises estimated risk.

## Modifiable levers
Blood pressure, LDL/HDL cholesterol, smoking cessation, weight/BMI, glycaemic
control, and preserving kidney function are the main modifiable contributors.
Higher HDL is protective. Risk rises steeply with age and systolic blood pressure.

## Interpreting the estimate
The output is an absolute probability, not a diagnosis. It supports shared
decisions about lipid-lowering therapy, blood-pressure targets, and lifestyle
change — always in the context of the whole patient.
