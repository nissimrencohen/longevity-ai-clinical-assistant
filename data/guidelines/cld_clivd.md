# Chronic Liver Disease Risk — CLivD score (summary)

*Simplified educational summary prepared for this exercise. Not clinical guidance.*
Source: CLivD score for incident chronic liver disease (general population).

## What it estimates
The ~15-year risk of a future severe liver event (e.g. cirrhosis, liver-related
hospitalisation) in the general population, using widely available inputs.

## Risk factors used
- **Age** and **sex**.
- **Alcohol use** (drinks per week) — a primary driver.
- **Waist-to-hip ratio** (central adiposity).
- **Diabetes**.
- **Smoking**.
- **Gamma-glutamyl transferase (GGT)** in the laboratory version — a marker of
  hepatic stress; higher values raise the estimate.

## Modifiable levers
Reducing alcohol intake, weight/central-adiposity reduction, glycaemic control,
and smoking cessation are the main modifiable contributors. A markedly elevated
GGT alongside high alcohol intake is a strong combined signal.

## Interpreting the estimate
A high score supports counselling on alcohol and metabolic risk, and consideration
of non-invasive fibrosis assessment (e.g. FIB-4) and follow-up.
