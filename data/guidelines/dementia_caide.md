# Dementia Risk — CAIDE score (summary)

*Simplified educational summary prepared for this exercise. Not clinical guidance.*
Source: Cardiovascular Risk Factors, Aging and Incidence of Dementia (CAIDE) score.

## What it estimates
The ~20-year risk of dementia estimated from midlife risk factors. It highlights
that cardiovascular and lifestyle factors in midlife shape later cognitive risk.

## Risk factors used
- **Age**.
- **Sex**.
- **Education** (fewer years of formal education raises risk).
- **Systolic blood pressure** (higher raises risk).
- **Body mass index (BMI)** (obesity raises risk).
- **Total cholesterol** (higher raises risk).
- **Physical activity** (being active lowers risk).

## Modifiable levers
Blood-pressure control, weight management, lipid management, and regular physical
activity in midlife are the main modifiable contributors. Age, sex, and education
are non-modifiable but inform baseline risk.

## Interpreting the estimate
This is a long-horizon, midlife-oriented estimate for risk communication and
motivating vascular-risk and lifestyle interventions — not a diagnostic tool.
