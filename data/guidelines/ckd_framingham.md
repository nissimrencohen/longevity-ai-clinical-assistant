# Chronic Kidney Disease Risk — Framingham CKD score (summary)

*Simplified educational summary prepared for this exercise. Not clinical guidance.*
Source: Framingham Heart Study 10-year CKD risk score.

## What it estimates
The 10-year risk of developing chronic kidney disease (stage 3+, i.e. a sustained
eGFR below 60 mL/min/1.73m²) in adults without known CKD.

## Risk factors used
- **Age**.
- **Diabetes**.
- **Hypertension**.
- **Proteinuria** — dipstick protein of *trace or above*.
- **Baseline kidney function (eGFR)** — a reduced eGFR raises risk sharply.

## Modifiable levers
Tight control of blood glucose and blood pressure, and treatment of albuminuria
/ proteinuria (e.g. renin-angiotensin blockade where appropriate), are the main
ways to slow progression. Diabetes and hypertension together are the dominant
drivers of incident CKD.

## Interpreting the estimate
Use alongside current eGFR and albuminuria trends. A rising estimate warrants
closer monitoring of kidney function and review of nephrotoxic exposures.
