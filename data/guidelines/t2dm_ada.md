# Type 2 Diabetes Risk — ADA Risk Test (summary)

*Simplified educational summary prepared for this exercise. Not clinical guidance.*
Source: American Diabetes Association Type 2 Diabetes Risk Test.

## What it estimates
A screening-level likelihood that an adult has or will develop type 2 diabetes,
using non-laboratory factors. A higher score flags the need for confirmatory
testing (HbA1c, fasting glucose, or an oral glucose tolerance test).

## Risk factors used
- **Age** (risk increases with age).
- **Sex**.
- **Body mass index (BMI)** — the strongest modifiable driver.
- **First-degree family history of diabetes**.
- **High blood pressure / hypertension**.
- **Physical inactivity** (being active lowers risk).
- **History of gestational diabetes** (in women).

## Modifiable levers
Weight loss, regular physical activity, and blood-pressure control meaningfully
reduce risk. Family history and age are non-modifiable but raise baseline risk.

## Interpreting the estimate
This is a screening signal, not a diagnosis. An elevated result should prompt a
glycaemic laboratory work-up rather than immediate treatment.
